## Bayesian Optimization for Organic Photovoltaic Processing

**Research question.** How can information from previous material systems support low-budget process optimization without assuming that the same relationships transfer unchanged to a new system?

**Project experience.** I have worked on transfer Bayesian optimization, virtual-target evaluation, ablation studies, negative-transfer analysis, statistical comparisons, and scientific visualization. The work connects structured data and explicit assumptions with reproducible computational comparisons and experimental-design decisions.

**Evaluation focus.** Clear baselines, appropriate comparisons, transparent analysis, and keeping virtual evaluation distinct from prospective experimental work.
